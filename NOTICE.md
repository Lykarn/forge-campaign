## Not an official product

Forge Campaign is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved or endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC.

It is a free, non-commercial hobby project: it is not sold, carries no ads and asks for no payment. Card images are not distributed with it; like Forge, it downloads them from Scryfall while you play.

## What it is made of

- **Forge** (https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge): the rules engine, the cards, the AI, the Adventure mode and everything in `res/`. GNU GPL v3.
- **Neo Forge** (https://github.com/AdrianLopez98/NeoForge): the JavaFX table, deck builder and the bridge that shows Adventure duels on that table. GNU GPL v3.
- **Forge Campaign** (https://github.com/Lykarn/forge-campaign): the campaign module and plane on top of both. As a derivative work it is released under the same licence, GNU GPL v3 (see `LICENSE`).

`SOURCE.txt` in every build names the exact commits it was built from.
