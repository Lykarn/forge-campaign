FORGE CAMPAIGN
==============

A reworked Adventure campaign for Magic: The Gathering, built on Forge's rules
engine and Neo Forge's table. Free, unofficial fan project (see NOTICE.txt).

To play
-------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere you can write to (not Program Files).
2. Run ForgeCampaign.exe.
3. In the menu, open Adventure. It starts on the Campaign plane.

The first launch takes about a minute while the engine reads every card.
Card images download the first time each card is shown.

Your data
---------
Saves, decks and settings live in the data folder next to ForgeCampaign.exe.
Move or copy the whole folder and your progress goes with it.

Reporting a problem
-------------------
Open an issue at https://github.com/Lykarn/forge-campaign/issues and attach
data/neo/neo.log and data/neo/adventure.log.
